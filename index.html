The short version

Most people in this field have done one of these things. I have done both.

Policy people who have actually passed legislation rarely ship production AI systems. People who ship AI systems rarely have to defend a rule in front of a national press corps. The interesting work sits where those two meet, and that is where I have spent the last three years.

01 — The intersection

Rules, then systems

A 100% priority bill passage rate as Assistant Secretary in the Office of the President — including national e-commerce regulation with content-governance and platform-accountability provisions. Then a live product on the Claude API whose guardrails, escalation thresholds and refusal behaviour I specified myself.

02 — The domain

Where AI harm lands first

A decade in the Philippines–Gulf migration corridor: the Passport Confiscation Matrix at Migrant Forum in Asia, a 2018 thesis on why Gulf states bypass their ILO commitments, and now The Canary in the Dragnet. Not a general interest in AI ethics — a specific, evidenced thesis about who gets surveilled first.

03 — The operator

Owning the outcome

Elected at 26 to a 12-municipality district. Then ran the executive–legislature interface for a national government. Now runs a responsible-AI company and an autonomous commerce operation solo — every function, every decision, no team.

What I'm building now

Four things, all pointed at the same question: what happens when an automated system makes a decision about someone who cannot argue back?

Founder & CEO · 2024–present

Handa

A responsible-AI legal-aid platform for overseas Filipino workers, built on the Claude API. I own the full safety surface: what the model may answer, what routes to a human, and what it must refuse outright — for users facing wage theft, passport confiscation and contract substitution with no legal recourse.

Read the guardrail architecture → Ongoing

WPS Maritime Tracker

An incident-to-award evidentiary tracker for the West Philippine Sea: each incident mapped to UNCLOS articles, COLREGs rules and holdings of the 2016 Arbitral Award, with an append-only chain of custody and a publication gate that only releases adjudicated records.

See the evidentiary spine → Founder & Operator · 2025–present

the Margins Studio

An autonomous storefront agent: Claude Vision analyses the artwork, the agent selects products, generates listings and publishes them. Between the model and the public sits gate.js — a hand-defined moderation layer that blocks what fails quality, brand and IP checks.

See the pipeline and the gate → Research · 2026

The Canary in the Dragnet

Original policy research arguing that migrants are the first population on whom AI surveillance infrastructure is normalised and tested — which makes migrant-rights failure a leading indicator of general AI governance failure. Submitted to the CHT Emerging Voices Fellowship.
