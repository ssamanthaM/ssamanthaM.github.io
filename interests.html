A passion project

Animal welfare in the Philippines

I sponsor and donate to animal-welfare associations in the Philippines, including the K9 Rehabilitation and Retirement Center — a sanctuary for retired working dogs and rescues.

Animal welfare in the Philippines is chronically under-funded and almost entirely dependent on private sponsorship. Shelters run on individual donors covering food, veterinary care and enclosure maintenance month to month, with no structural funding underneath them and no constituency lobbying on their behalf.

It is the same shape as the rest of my work: a population with an obvious claim on decent treatment, no standing to make that claim itself, and a system that only responds when somebody with standing insists. I would rather be one of the people insisting.
