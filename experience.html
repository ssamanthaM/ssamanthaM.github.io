Ongoing
Independent build

WPS Maritime Tracker

Incident-to-award evidentiary tracker for the West Philippine Sea

  • Built the evidentiary spine for a decade of dispersed West Philippine Sea evidence: 16 structured case records across Ayungin, Bajo de Masinloc, Escoda and Iroquois Reef, each mapping the act to specific UNCLOS articles, COLREGs rules and holdings of the 2016 South China Sea Arbitral Award, with Berkeley Protocol-informed provenance, source tiering and disclosable methodology.
  • Designed a tamper-evident chain of custody — an append-only hash chain where each entry commits to its own row and the previous entry's hash, with updates and deletes blocked at the database and digests anchored via OpenTimestamps. Evidence with an empty custody log is flagged not filing-ready rather than silently shown.
  • Made disclosure a whitelist, not a judgement call: a record is public only if it is adjudicated stable and its feature is in an active corpus phase, and only views explicitly named as public may be served by a route.
  • Put the LLM behind the gate. A Claude-driven pipeline structures open-source reporting into the schema, but every record it produces enters as an unverified draft that cannot reach the published view without human adjudication — the same discipline as Handa, applied to evidence rather than advice.
  • Corroborates from independent data: automatic Global Fishing Watch correlation of encounter, loitering and AIS-gap events within ±7 days and 75 km — surfacing, for one 2024 boarding, 250+ militia-linked vessels loitering within 9 km on the same day.
UNCLOS / COLREGsChain of custody Berkeley ProtocolCloudflare Workers Human-in-the-loopOSINT
Why this sits next to the AI work rather than apart from it

Because it is the same governance problem with the stakes moved. Handa decides what a model may tell a worker with no lawyer. This decides what a model-assisted record may claim in something that could end up in front of counsel. In both, the interesting engineering is the boundary — and in both, the boundary is enforced by the data model rather than by anyone's good intentions.

It is also the only project on this list that closes a loop with my own legislative record: I negotiated maritime rules on these features at the ASEAN Inter-Parliamentary Assembly, and this is the evidentiary system for the dispute those rules were meant to govern.

What running a district actually teaches you

That a policy is only as good as the person who has to administer it in a municipality with three staff and intermittent connectivity. That the people most affected by a decision are almost never in the room where it is made. And that if you cannot explain a rule to the person it lands on, in their language, you have not finished writing it.

Those three lessons are the reason I now work on AI systems that make decisions about people who have no way to contest them.
