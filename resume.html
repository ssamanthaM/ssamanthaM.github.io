Research & applied AI

  • Handa — model guardrails & escalation. Specified what the model may answer, what routes to a human reviewer and what it must refuse outright, for users facing wage theft, passport confiscation and contract substitution with no legal recourse. Prompt design, output evaluation for accuracy and bias, and an escalation path built on the assumption the model will sometimes be confidently wrong.
  • WPS Maritime Tracker — incident-to-award evidentiary system. 16 structured case records of West Philippine Sea incidents, each mapped to UNCLOS articles, COLREGs rules and holdings of the 2016 South China Sea Arbitral Award, with Berkeley Protocol-informed provenance, an append-only SHA-256 custody chain anchored via OpenTimestamps, and a publication whitelist that releases only adjudicated records. A Claude pipeline structures open-source reporting into the schema; every record it produces enters as an unverified draft requiring human adjudication before publication. Built for the Solicitor General's office, Coast Guard legal and international counsel.
  • the Margins Studio — autonomous agent with a moderation gate. Full pipeline from Claude Vision analysis to automated publishing, with a pre-publish enforcement layer and a weekly evaluation loop. Practical experience with the core agentic-safety problem.
  • “The Canary in the Dragnet” (2026). Original policy research arguing migrants are the first population on whom AI surveillance infrastructure is normalised and tested, making migrant-rights failure a leading indicator of general AI governance failure. Submitted to the CHT Emerging Voices Fellowship. Builds on the Passport Confiscation Matrix and a 2018 Paris thesis on Gulf states' ILO circumvention.
  • SolBound — incentive design prototype. Reward mechanics and abuse-resistance model for a sober-to-earn recovery application: rewarding a behaviour only the user can verify, without creating an exploit that falls hardest on the intended beneficiaries.
